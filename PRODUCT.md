# Cyber Shift
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Thai-speaking players learning cybersecurity. Mac, iPhone and iPad are confirmed targets.
## Product Purpose
Learn attack recognition and response by playing an employee in a simulated office. The long-term brief includes Red Team and Blue Team at multiple difficulties.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing buildless HTML/JS app, public Cloudflare Pages and Sites deployments, offline Mac WKWebView wrapper, local saves. This release has three playable incidents (phishing, ransomware response, and a Trojan disguised as an ordinary app), a simulated employee workstation with four desktop shortcuts and a five-app catalog, 10–20 longer mixed messages per case delivered gradually, a phishing-link consequence and recovery screen, case-specific SOC events, and an optional client-authorized Red Team exercise from scope to report. The browser, AI, IP monitor, login page, download, and test target are simulations. No real targets, credentials, malware, or attacks. No music or sound effects.
## Brand Commitments
Cyber Shift. The user explicitly requests a cool, semi-fairytale world, actual employee models, Sims-like play, and less text. Preserve Thai voice. Impeccable and Ponytail are requested.
## Product Principles
Show consequences in the world. Let the player act before explaining. Keep controls usable with keyboard and touch. Preserve previous saves without overwriting their storage key.
## Open decisions
Broader campaign, real multiplayer and native iOS packaging remain future work.

Default play is free exploration with independent selection of all three incidents. Guided cases remain optional. Store and website app sources both include ordinary choices; only voluntarily launched risky website software triggers the simulated Trojan SOC event. Free SOC shifts now sample optional cases and progress a foreign-login investigation (legitimate travel or intrusion) and repeated password failures. Nara Guard can scan and contain the suspicious app; an uncontained launch progresses to outbound traffic and simulated document exfiltration.

The workstation also has a simulated Nara Update page. A small dismissible owl coaching card explains phishing, Trojan scanning, true/false-positive foreign logins, and brute force responses after relevant player actions. SOC pulses before a repeated-login incident is established.

Terminal Lab now offers non-executing macOS, Windows and Linux commands and fixed Nmap examples against documentation IP 192.0.2.10. Certificate lesson shows live origin/protocol and directs users to the browser’s actual certificate viewer; phishing-domain certificate details remain explicitly simulated.

Guided cases now provide progressive contextual hints at 60 and 120 seconds per stage across all 17 stages, visible in the office and active dialogs, paused when the game is paused or tab is hidden. A QR PNG links to the stable Cloudflare Pages URL.
